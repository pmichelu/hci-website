This is a list of online volunteers (aka “Catchers”) who played Stall Catchers to analyze Alzheimer’s research data for the Schaffer-Nishimura Laboratory in the Biomedical Engineering Department, Cornell University. The analysis provided by these Catchers was used to answer research questions concerning the effect of NOX inhibitors on the frequency of stalls in Alzheimer’s mice. You can learn more about the dataset here.
